Systematic Review Protocol

Incretin Agonists in HFpEF + Obesity — Continuous Outcomes (Mean Difference)

PICO Framework

Population Adults with HFpEF (LVEF ≥45–50%) and obesity (BMI ≥30 kg/m²), with or without type 2 diabetes
Intervention GLP-1 receptor agonist (semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly) or dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist (tirzepatide 15 mg weekly)
Comparator Placebo (matching subcutaneous injection)
Primary Outcome KCCQ-CSS (Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire — Clinical Summary Score): change from baseline (mean difference, points)
Secondary Outcomes Body weight change (%), 6-minute walk distance (metres), worsening heart failure events (composite: HF hospitalization or urgent HF visit)
Subgroup Plan Drug mechanism (GLP-1 RA only vs dual GLP-1/GIP RA), diabetes status (no DM vs DM)

Search Strategy

Database Search Approach Date Range
ClinicalTrials.gov Condition: "Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction" OR "HFpEF"; Intervention: semaglutide OR tirzepatide OR liraglutide OR GLP-1; Phase 3; Status: completed or has results 2020 – 2026
PubMed / MEDLINE (GLP-1 OR semaglutide OR tirzepatide OR incretin) AND (HFpEF OR "heart failure preserved") AND (randomized OR RCT); filtered to clinical trial publications 2020 – 2026
Manual / Reference Backward citation search of included trials and relevant meta-analyses; conference abstracts (AHA, ESC, ACC) for unpublished results No limit

Eligibility Criteria

What does this analysis show?

Incretin-based weight-loss medicines (semaglutide and tirzepatide) significantly improve heart failure symptoms and quality of life in patients with HFpEF and obesity.

Symptom improvement (KCCQ) was clinically meaningful across all 3 trials.

GRADE Certainty Assessment

Sensitivity Tornado

One-way sensitivity: how the pooled MD shifts under each scenario vs. the base-case estimate. Green = CI still excludes null; red = crosses null.

GOSH Plot

All 2^k study subsets. Each point = one subset's pooled MD vs I². Clusters reveal hidden heterogeneity structures.

RoB Heatmap

Cochrane RoB 2.0 heatmap. Hover for domain justifications. Green=low, yellow=some concerns, red=high risk.

Doi Plot (LFK Index)

Normal quantile plot of study effects. LFK index quantifies asymmetry: |LFK|<1 none, 1-2 minor, ≥2 major.

PET-PEESE

Bias-adjusted pooled estimate. PET regresses effect on SE; PEESE on SE². Conditional: use PEESE if PET slope is significant.

P-curve Analysis

Distribution of significant p-values. Right-skewed = genuine effect; uniform = no evidential value; left-skewed = p-hacking concern.

Meta-Regression

Bubble plot: effect vs. % diabetes. Size = precision (1/SE²). Line = WLS regression. Exploratory with k=3.

Influence Diagnostics Panel

Viechtbauer & Cheung (2010) framework. Dashed lines = threshold values. Studies outside thresholds may unduly influence the pooled result.

Fragility Index

Walsh et al. (2014). Events needed to flip significance per study. Lower = more fragile. FQ = FI / total N.

Excess Significance

Ioannidis & Trikalinos (2007). Compares observed vs. expected significant results given study powers. O >> E suggests selective reporting.

Drapery Plot (P-value Functions)

Rücker & Schwarzer (2020). Each curve = one study's p-value function. Thick = pooled. Horizontal lines at α = 0.05, 0.01. Where curves cross = CI bounds at that level.

About This Review

Statistical Methodology

Primary Engine

DerSimonian-Laird (DL) random-effects model for continuous outcomes (mean difference). Inverse-variance weighting with moment-based tau-squared estimation.

Effect measure: Mean Difference (MD) with 95% CI. For body weight, expressed as percentage change. For KCCQ-CSS and 6MWD, expressed in original units (points and metres).

Sensitivity Methods

HKSJ adjustment: Hartung-Knapp-Sidik-Jonkman uses t-distribution with k-1 degrees of freedom, providing better coverage than DL for small k.

Bayesian grid approximation: N(0, 900) prior on pooled MD (SD=30); half-normal prior on tau. Grid resolution: 400 mu x 300 tau points. Reports posterior mean and 95% credible interval.

Heterogeneity

I²: Percentage of variability due to between-study heterogeneity (0% = homogeneous, >75% = considerable).

Prediction interval: Expected range for the true effect in a future similar study. Particularly important with k = 3 trials.

Indirect Comparison

Bucher method: Anchored indirect comparison of semaglutide vs tirzepatide via shared placebo comparator. Valid only under consistency assumption.

Worsening HF: Peto odds ratio used for this sparse binary outcome. Pooled by Mantel-Haenszel when events permit.
